Privacy Policy — Legendary Kingdom

Information We Collect

We collect your chosen player name. If you sign in via Google or Facebook, we save your email to contact you if issues arise. We also record sign-up dates, login counts, play time, device platform, app version, and in-game state.

Why We Use It

To run your account, save progress, prevent cheating and duplicate names, improve the game, and contact you if needed.

Data Retention

Login logs are kept for about 13 months, and daily activity for about 2 years. Core account data remains as long as your account is active.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is stored securely via Supabase, and Google/Facebook are used strictly for authentication.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account inside the game (Settings → Delete Account) or via the Account Deletion Page. Accounts are permanently deleted 30 days after the request and can be restored by logging in before then.
